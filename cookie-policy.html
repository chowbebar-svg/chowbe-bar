Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy explains how Chowbe Bar (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on chowbebar.com, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to site owners. We also use similar technologies such as local storage, which works in much the same way. In this policy we refer to all of these simply as “cookies”.

The cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These are required for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) we do not need your consent to set them, because you have asked for a service that cannot be provided without them.

Name Purpose Duration
chowbe_cookie_consent Remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies, so we do not ask again on every visit. Until you clear your browser storage
Theme interface cookies Set by the website template to remember interface state, such as menus and tabs, while you browse. Session

Optional analytics cookies

We would like to set optional cookies that help us understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve it. These are only set if you give consent by choosing “Accept optional cookies” on our cookie banner.

At present we do not run any analytics on this site, so no optional cookies are actually set. If we introduce them in future, this policy will be updated and you will be asked for consent before any such cookie is placed on your device.

Third party services

Some services we rely on may set their own cookies or process data when you interact with certain parts of the site:

  • Web3Forms — processes the enquiry form so your message reaches us. It is only contacted when you submit the form.
  • Netlify — hosts and delivers this website. It may set cookies that are strictly necessary for security and delivery.

These providers operate under their own privacy and cookie policies, which we recommend you review.

How to manage your choices

You can change your mind at any time. Select Cookie Settings in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and update your choice. Rejecting optional cookies will not limit your use of the site in any way.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting cookies already stored and blocking new ones. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site working as intended. Guidance for common browsers is available from your browser provider, and the Information Commissioner’s Office publishes general advice for UK users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the cookies we use or to legal requirements. Any changes will appear on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
